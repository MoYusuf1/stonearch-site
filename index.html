FAQ

Asked Often, Answered Plainly

What does it cost, total?

$499 once for the website, then $49/mo for care — $793 total for the build plus the first 6 months of care. Domain registration, if you need one, is about $13/yr at cost.

How fast can my site be live?

Our target is 2–3 days from the day we have your menu/services, photos, and hours — the template does the heavy lifting.

What does the $49/mo care include?

Hosting management, keeping the site up and secure, and up to 2 small edits a month. Need more? Unlimited edits is +$50/mo.

Why a 6-month minimum on care?

The $499 build price is low because care keeps the site maintained. Six months keeps it fair on both sides; after that it’s month-to-month. (The $0-down agencies lock you in for 12.)

What if I cancel inside 6 months?

The contract sets it out before you sign: either the remaining care months or a site buyout. Nothing is improvised per client.

I have a Square or Linktree page already

That’s a starting point, not a website you own. We build your real site and link your ordering and socials into it.
