FAQ

Asked often,
answered plainly.

What does it cost, total?

$499 once for the website, then $49/mo for care — $793 total for the build plus the first 6 months of care. Add-ons are one-off at the prices above. Domain registration, if you need one, is about $13/yr, billed at cost plus a small management fee.

How fast can my site be live?

Our target is 2–3 days from the day we have your menu/services, photos, and hours — the template does the heavy lifting.

What does the $49/mo care actually include?

Hosting management, keeping the site up and secure, and up to 2 small edits a month (text, photos, hours, prices). New pages or redesigns are quoted separately.

Why a 6-month minimum on care?

The $499 build price is low because care keeps the site maintained over time. Six months keeps it fair on both sides; after that it's month-to-month and you can cancel.

What if I need to cancel inside the first 6 months?

The contract sets it out before you sign: either the remaining care months or a site buyout. Nothing is improvised per client.

How do we communicate?

Email only, on purpose. No calls or meetings — you send changes when it suits you, edits are batched on set days each week, and everything is in writing.

What if I already have a Square or Linktree page?

That counts as our starting point, not a website you own. We build your real site and link your Square ordering and socials into it.
